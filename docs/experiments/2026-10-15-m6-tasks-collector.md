policy,256/32/16 category caps and literal grading rules. Actual Online
attention and selected small compensated Half/larger frozen Q4 v2 modes are
required. Both reviewed10000-target loss gates are prerequisites. The accepted

original/M6 contracts and tokenizer assets are pinned and reasserted immediately

exact original caps, raw event/response replay, ordered native-memory coverage,
natural owned exit and independent finalized text. The existing literal Linux
window retains its repeated process/group absence checks, qualified image and
controller, unresolved-case policy and initially-stopped VM restoration.
