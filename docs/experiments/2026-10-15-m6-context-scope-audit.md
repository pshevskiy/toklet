four real collections and 5,652 raw events. This qualifies the checker against
partial evidence; the complete 32-record context matrix remains pending.

4K/8K/16K/32K depth and quality/profile/long/long-confirmation plan: exactly 32
roles. It uses the accepted auditor to replay every request, state, raw event,
memory charge, identity and derived metric. The bytes parsed for synthesis must
have the same SHA as those replayed. Its own and the reused quality checker's
source identities are captured before work and verified at the final endpoint.
