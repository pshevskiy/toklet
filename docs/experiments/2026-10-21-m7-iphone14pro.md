## Latency, sustained behavior and memory

The [repeated context matrix](2026-10-21-m7-phone-context.md) retains two runs per
model/prompt, with three warmups and ten measured requests per run: 156 requests
and 19,968 visible events. At prompt length 512, native TTFT medians are
10.846/15.058 seconds small and 49.883/48.466 seconds larger; decode medians are
15.638/12.756 and 3.613/3.649 IDs/s respectively. The report preserves all other
prompt lengths, raw samples, dispersion and complete-request times. These are
descriptive observations from a heated, wired device, not controlled speedup,
p99, isolated-prefill or energy measurements.

The [dedicated small sustained sequence](2026-10-21-m7-phone-small-sustained.md)
contains three warmups plus 24 measured 512/128 requests, totaling
620.5660416229999 completed native seconds excluding warmups. Every 128-ID
sequence repeats. First/last-five decode medians fall from 15.511 to 8.386 IDs/s;
fair/serious thermal states and the slowdown remain part of the result. Larger
512-token context runs already contain 839.172/827.973 measured native seconds;
1,024-token runs contain 1,344.731/1,329.965 seconds. Sustained duration does not
imply a constant throughput rate or an independently controlled thermal cause.

The largest uninstrumented observed whole-process lifetime peak across the joined
trials is 1,944,701,920 bytes, within the explicit 2.5 GiB trial cap. State/lifecycle
peaks reach 1,114,360,224 small and 1,929,808,864 larger. These are measured process
observations, including the harness/framework resources reported by task_info;
numerical admission remains a distinct logical count. Memory pressure, other
applications and OS policy can change available headroom. The Mac 6 GiB budget
is not used as a phone allowance.

actual Power Profiler attachment attempts: verified PID then process name after
an exact unique-PID guard. They fail with exits21 and19 respectively, with no trace or attributable units;

natural process absence. Energy is unavailable in this tested setup and remains
unset. No joules, watts, battery-life or efficiency claim follows from a template
listing, a relative score or an unsuccessful attachment. The cause of the tool
failure and any general hardware limitation are not established.

difference adds the reviewed scalar diagnostic C boundary and its tests; numerical

quality collection and the final state app. Later app changes add bounded state
witnesses and correct successful close publication; the final host-only argv fix
does not change signed app bytes. Exact artifact/source/installation hashes and
failed attempts remain in each linked qualification.
